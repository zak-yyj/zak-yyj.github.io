### Assessing Multi-Source Optical and Machine-Learning Methods for Southwest Greenland Supraglacial Lake Evolution

*MSc Dissertation, University of Glasgow*

- Investigated the spatial and temporal evolution of supraglacial lakes in Southwest Greenland using multi-source optical satellite imagery.
- Combined Sentinel-2 and Landsat data with spectral indices and Random Forest classification to map supraglacial lakes.
- Evaluated mapping performance and analysed the spatial and temporal patterns of lake evolution.

### Greenland Glacier Surface Temperature Retrieval Using Satellite Remote Sensing

*BSc Dissertation, Nanjing Xiaozhuang University*

- Investigated glacier surface temperature across Greenland using Landsat satellite imagery.
- Applied a radiative transfer equation approach to retrieve glacier surface temperature and examined its spatial and temporal variation.
- Validated the retrieval results using automatic weather station observations and assessed major sources of uncertainty.
