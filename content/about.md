I have a background in Geographic Information Science and Geospatial and Mapping Sciences, with research interests in **GeoAI, remote sensing, cartography, and data-driven geospatial analysis**.

My recent research has focused on applying machine learning and multi-source optical satellite data to environmental monitoring. My MSc dissertation examined **supraglacial lake evolution in Southwest Greenland** using Sentinel-2 and Landsat imagery, spectral indices, and machine-learning methods. My undergraduate dissertation investigated **Greenland glacier surface temperature** using satellite remote sensing data.

I am interested in developing data-driven geospatial methods for environmental and spatial analysis, particularly through the integration of remote sensing, machine learning, and spatial information. High-latitude and polar regions are an important application area of my research, while my broader interests extend to GeoAI and geospatial analysis more generally.
