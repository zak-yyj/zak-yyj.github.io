I am currently pursuing an MSc in Geospatial \& Mapping Sciences, with a strong

research interest in \*\*GeoAI and the integration of artificial intelligence

with geospatial data analysis\*\*.



My academic focus lies in applying machine learning and data-driven approaches

to remote sensing and mapping problems, particularly for high-latitude and

polar regions. I am interested in how GeoAI methods can be used to improve

the accuracy, efficiency, and scalability of geospatial analysis, including

tasks such as surface parameter retrieval, spatial pattern recognition,

and multi-source data integration.



My long-term research goal is to develop GeoAI-based methodologies that bridge

remote sensing, cartography, and spatial accuracy assessment, with applications

in environmental monitoring and cryospheric studies.
