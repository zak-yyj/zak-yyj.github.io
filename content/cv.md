## Research Experience

**Assessing Multi-Source Optical and Machine-Learning Methods for Southwest Greenland Supraglacial Lake Evolution**  
*MSc Dissertation, University of Glasgow*

- Investigated the spatial and temporal evolution of supraglacial lakes in Southwest Greenland.
- Integrated Sentinel-2 and Landsat optical imagery using spectral indices and machine-learning methods.
- Evaluated lake mapping performance and interpreted patterns of supraglacial lake development.

**Greenland Glacier Surface Temperature Retrieval Using Satellite Remote Sensing**  
*BSc Dissertation, Nanjing Xiaozhuang University*

- Investigated glacier surface temperature across Greenland using satellite remote sensing data.
- Applied a radiative transfer equation approach to retrieve land surface temperature from Landsat imagery.
- Analysed spatial and temporal variation and validated results using automatic weather station observations.
